# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Fix flickering where open saddlebag lids meet an animal's torso.
- Keep the lids visible on Minecraft 26 kittens and pups, standing and sitting.
- Improve saddlebag positioning with Fat Cat and Fresh Animations, including enlarged kitten heads and the wolf mane.
- Preserve support for Minecraft 1.21.9, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, NeoForge and Forge.

Animated model packs can still produce occasional paw contacts with the bags. The original Fat Cat pack may require updated pack metadata on modern Minecraft.

## 1.0.0

- Continue Chested Companions with Ender and copper saddlebags, copper golem interaction and chest removal.
- Port the shared Stonecutter project to all six Minecraft versions and all three loaders.
