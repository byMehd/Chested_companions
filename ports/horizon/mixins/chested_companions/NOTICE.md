Chested Companions: Revived for Horizon

Native Rust adaptation by byMehd, based on the MIT-licensed Chested Companions
by Khajiitos and Chested Companions: Revived.

Source: https://github.com/byMehd/Chested_companions
Original: https://github.com/Khajiitos/ChestedCompanions

The bundled saddlebag textures are from the mod's MIT-licensed source tree.
Minecraft assets and third-party resource packs are not included.
